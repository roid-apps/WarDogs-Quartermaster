## Prepare for your next deployment

Find the equipment you use, build a purchase list and check how often you can afford to buy it again. Keep a personal cash reserve and see the difference between recurring purchases and one-time unlock fees.

| Tool | What it helps you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Equipment catalog** | Search a dated community reference, compare items, save favorites and filter by unit price. Missing prices stay clearly unknown. |
| **Loadout & cargo budgets** | Plan quantities, confirm ammunition sale units, record owned unlocks and save kits without spending anything in your ledger. |
| **Cash planning** | Record your balance, purchases and rewards; compare repeat-kit costs against your reserve. |
| **Player field guide** | Start with a question about equipment, objectives, economy, logistics or progression and find the relevant tool or reference. |

![Equipment catalog with categories, price filters and favorites](https://github.com/roid-apps/WarDogs-Quartermaster/releases/download/v0.3.0/catalog.png)

## Give your squad a shared plan

Create a room, invite friends and coordinate in one place. See who is connected to the app, their preferred role, selected kit and readiness. Use text chat, a shared briefing and objectives to agree on the next deployment.

- **Choose what you share.** Cash and K/D/A sharing are optional.
- **Plan responsibilities.** Use notes, tasks and manual board markers for rally points, transport and supply duties.
- **Prepare construction.** Arrange symbolic base pieces and review supply totals by construction stage.
- **Stay in control.** Shared board changes are explicitly published or loaded, with conflicting edits flagged.

![Two demo squad members coordinating in a shared room](https://github.com/roid-apps/WarDogs-Quartermaster/releases/download/v0.3.0/squad.png)

*Internet rooms need a running HTTPS relay or tunnel configured on every friend's app. A built-in local test relay is included; localhost alone only works on the same computer. Rooms and chat are temporary. Presence means connected to Quartermaster, not detected inside the game. The example invite above belonged to a stopped test room.*

## Keep something useful from each match

Record **kills, deaths and assists** individually, or enter the scoreboard totals once after the match. Add what helped the team and one thing to change next time—support, transport and logistics count too. Your latest takeaway appears on the overview.

Session history, recorded cash flow, manual XP goals and JSON backups help you keep your own progress organized. A compact desktop view puts the essentials in a smaller window. Choose a background and accent color, or keep the default pure black.

## Download and get started

1. **[Download WarDogs-Quartermaster-0.3.0.exe](https://github.com/roid-apps/WarDogs-Quartermaster/releases/download/v0.3.0/WarDogs-Quartermaster-0.3.0.exe)** and open it. This is a portable application, not a setup wizard.
2. Open **settings-and-trust** to choose your display name, preferred role, reserve and colors.
3. Enter your actual cash balance in **overview**, or go straight to **armory-catalog** and **player-field-guide** to browse.
4. Prepare a kit, record a match or create a squad plan. Use only the parts that help you.

When updating, export a JSON backup in Settings and close the older app first. Existing local records carry forward.

## What to expect from this preview

**Your records are manual.** Quartermaster does not detect K/D/A, purchases, teammates or game state. Compact view is an ordinary desktop window; no in-game overlay or automatic capture is included.

**References have dates.** Equipment prices use a September 22, 2026 community snapshot. A September 25 index check hides six no-longer-listed entries by default; it did not refresh every price. Verify current prices, unlocks and compatibility in game. Boards are planning schematics, not game terrain or validated inventory packing. Detailed maps and 3D tools open on their reference websites.

**A separate desktop companion.** Quartermaster helps with planning and manual recordkeeping. It has no game-memory access, injection or gameplay automation. The Windows executable is unsigned.

**Checks performed:** 41 automated tests and four packaged Windows UI suites passed, plus Windows/Linux automated checks on GitHub. These checks do not establish live-game compatibility or separate-computer internet hosting reliability.
