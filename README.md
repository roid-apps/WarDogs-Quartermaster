## A proper place for every part of your kit

Click any equipment slot to open its searchable picker on the right. Choose a pictured item to equip it immediately. Primary weapon, sidearm and special equipment sit above helmet, armor and vest, with backpack, traversal and supplies alongside. The drawer groups choices by class and shows what is equipped. Each supported weapon has sight, muzzle, magazine and grip slots with compatible choices. Hover items for reference stats, inspect attachment handling, and plan loaded magazines. The purchase total updates as you build.

![Visual loadout builder](https://github.com/roid-apps/WarDogs-Quartermaster/releases/download/v0.7.0/loadout-builder.png)

Plan real backpack space with separate pockets, stack limits, drag-to-move, rotation and weapon slings. Items that do not fit remain visible in **Unpacked**. Carried weight shows the source weight bracket and its movement/handling effects; unavailable masses are named and make the result provisional. New physical stacks use the source sale unit, including ammunition packs. Existing shopping lists and manual prices remain editable. Saving a kit does not deduct cash. Weightless ammunition is counted as 0 kg, matching the source planner. Its footprint, stack limit and purchase cost still apply. Other unavailable masses remain flagged.

![Organized supplies with a large item preview](https://github.com/roid-apps/WarDogs-Quartermaster/releases/download/v0.7.0/loadout-supplies.png)

## Plan on the terrain

Explore **Bakurani, Ozeti and Zestafona** with deep cursor-centered zoom, zone search and expandable map view. Toggle towers, settlements, industry, terrain, control circles, faction bases and facilities. Select the match’s control-zone placement to distinguish contested towers. Add roads, relief, labels or a 1 km grid; measure straight-line distance and bearing. Place your own rally points and route waypoints, then publish the plan to your squad.

![Tactical board with terrain and squad markers](https://github.com/roid-apps/WarDogs-Quartermaster/releases/download/v0.7.0/tactical-map.png)

Maps and dated factual layers come from [Wardogs Zone](https://wardogs.zone/maps), with original watermarks retained. Overviews and layers work offline; first-time deep zoom downloads and caches original detail tiles. The **High-detail tiles** switch controls these requests. Planning is manual, with no live player tracking. Vehicle routing, ETA and fuel simulation remain linked to the source calculator.

## Build a clearer base plan

Choose from an illustrated palette of 25 construction pieces. Click to place, drag to move, rotate, duplicate, snap to the grid and undo changes. Organize construction into stages and see the source-listed supply total. A compact inspector keeps the canvas in view.

![Base planner with illustrated structures](https://github.com/roid-apps/WarDogs-Quartermaster/releases/download/v0.7.0/base-planner.png)

Structure sizes are symbolic. Terrain collision, physical footprints, stacking and build restrictions are not simulated. The full external 3D builder remains linked from the Field manual.

## Make the workspace yours

Choose your own profile picture in **Settings → Your operator identity**. It appears in your dock, squad roster and messages while you are in the room. Change the background and accent colors, or turn off the subtle terrain artwork for pure black.

## Host a space for your team

Open **Team server**, name your team and choose **Start hosting**. Friends paste the invite into **Join a team**. The app can download the free Cloudflare helper, or use an existing installation.

- Up to **16 members**, with dynamic presence, roles, selected loadouts and readiness.
- Chat, briefing, objectives and shared boards; the host saves the workspace between sessions.
- Lock new joins, remove members, copy the invite or stop hosting in the app.
- Cash and K/D/A sharing are optional. Profile pictures are shared when joining a room.

The host's app and computer must stay online. Free Cloudflare Quick Tunnels have no uptime guarantee and use a new address after restart; saved team content remains. Share a fresh invite when resuming. Use version 0.7.0 for the host/relay and guests to share the new map and avatar fields.

## Download and run

1. Download the Windows EXE above and open it.
2. Set your name, role and picture in Settings.
3. Open Loadout workshop to build a kit, or Tactical board to plan on a map.
4. Use Team server when you want to plan with friends.

The EXE is unsigned. It is a standalone companion with manual records: no game-memory reading, input automation, screen capture, Steam sign-in or automatic combat tracking. A game overlay is not included. The app does not automatically download new releases; download a newer EXE when updating.

Personal data stays in the app's local data folder, with backup export/restore in Settings. Hosted team content has a separate file and previous-copy backup. See [Privacy](PRIVACY.md) for what a room shares and stores.

The armory price catalog retains its **September 22, 2026 snapshot** or your overrides. Physical inventory metadata, attachment purchases, stack sale units and map layers use the **September 25, 2026 Wardogs Zone reference**. Missing non-ammunition weights stay unknown. Attachment totals are labeled as a modeled preview; check current game values after patches.

Screenshots use isolated demo profiles and example plans, not live gameplay or personal records.
